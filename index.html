05 — Methodology

Mementary Policy Framework

MONIA

Meme Overnight Index Average

Open-interest-weighted annualised funding across liquid meme perpetuals. Funding is what it costs to hold leveraged long exposure for a day, so MONIA is the meme economy's overnight benchmark — its SONIA.

Memeflation

Debasement of the meme unit

Sector capitalisation divided by tokens in circulation gives real value per meme. Its rate of decline is inflation, decomposed on a matched sample into issuance and price components so that threshold crossings are recorded as entries and exits rather than mistaken for debasement.

The Bonk Rate

Policy rate · set by rule, not discretion

A Taylor rule with a neutral rate of 4.20% and a memeflation target of 2.00%. The degeneracy gap measures sector turnover against its own trailing trend — the output gap of the meme economy.

r* + π + 0.25(π − π*) + 0.25(degeneracy gap)

The Universe

Inclusion rule

All meme tokens at or above $1M market capitalisation, drawn from a fixed daily sample frame of 5,000 tokens. Constant scan depth means a change in the constituent count reflects a real threshold crossing, not a sampling artefact.

Monetary Aggregates

M0 · M1 · M2

M0, the Bonk Base, is stablecoin supply on Solana — the settlement asset of the meme economy. M1 adds the ten largest memes. M2, Broad Bonk, is the entire qualifying universe. Velocity is turnover divided by capitalisation.

The Corridor

Standing facilities

Facilities operate at the Bonk Rate ±100bp. When MONIA trades outside that band, transmission is impaired: the market is charging a materially different price for leverage than the Committee's stance implies.
